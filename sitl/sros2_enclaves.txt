# SROS 2 enclaves of the ROS 2 rail (the grant's dev graph), one per process.
#
# Read by tools/sros2_setup.sh (creates them in the keystore), by
# sitl/sros2_env.sh (refuses SROS2=1 when one is missing, and gives each
# process its own) and by tests/test_ros2_package.py (every process the
# launch file and sitl/run_ros2_demo.sh start under SROS2=1 has one here).
# The three Guardrail nodes are named after their launch processes
# (guardrail_rail.launch.py: safety_shield, mavlink_adapter, vla_stub).

/guardrail/mavlink_adapter
/guardrail/vla_stub
/guardrail/mavros
/guardrail/bag_recorder
