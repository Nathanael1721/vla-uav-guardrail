## C2 on hil: the Project AirSim follow

C2 was added on 16 November 2026, after the lab named AIoT-VLA v10b for the follow task on 15 November 2026. It was added because that choice rests on a follow, and C1 is a GOTO to a coordinate that a follow-only model cannot fly. A test written after the choice it bears on is not a prediction: C2 describes the chosen backend, and compares it only with backends that flew the same test.

Scenario: the Project AirSim follow: 'follow a red car' in CityLife under the perception node's default signed bundle (follow_car_citylife), cruise altitude 8 m; the mission clock starts when the start gate passes. With --identity, as C2 flies, the gate is follow_vlm.Reacquirer with no anchor: 10 identity-ok boxes in a row within 30 m (twice the node's --start-when-seen 5), of one object (each within 3 m + 3 m/s x the time since the last pick) and seen moving, an inference with no acceptable box or a box of another place restarting the streak, a place seen not to move refused; its streak and refusals are metrics.json start_gate.identity_gate. A run flown with --identity whose metrics.json start_gate records no `rule` was flown before the node stated this rule: its [start] line printed the pixel gate's streak and a timeout's end_reason reads '5 inferences in a row', though the rule above is the one that decided. Rail: hil: Project AirSim and ArduPilot SITL on the desktop; MAVROS 2, the perception node (demo/pas_ardupilot/perception_node.py --pilot vla --mission follow), the Shield and the VLA server on the Jetson Orin. Launch: `deploy/orin/run_pas_perception.sh FAMILY=<family>` with `--identity --negative-query a traffic light --target-estimator`, 420 s. A flight is C2 when: KPI-grade by the run's own kpi.json (hil, sim_speedup 1, signed bundle, clean commit); no perception stressor, no policy hot-applied in flight, the scenario's own instruction (not a paraphrase); --identity and --target-estimator on, the start gate at the node's defaults (--start-when-seen 5, --start-max-range-m 30), the family's feed, and for the stream feed a FrontCamera capture interval of 0.05 s, each as the run's params record it; the negative query ['a traffic light'] in the run's params, and the node's default signed bundle (follow-car-citylife-v<version>) as the run's policy_source; flown alone: nothing else of the lab's running on the desktop or the Orin (no run folder records this; the flights known not to be are listed by hand in tools/vla_backend_table.py C2_NOT_ALONE); the start gate passed (a flight that never started has no mission to score; one that would be C2 but for a gate that timed out is censored: shown beside its backend's C2 flights as 'gate timed out N: censored, not scored, not counted toward the 3', so a backend's n is never silently smaller; the protocol flies another in its place, and the C2 count against the 3 says whether that is done); the 420 s limit, by the limit the run's mission monitor records it was given: a flight given that limit is C2 however it ended, one given another is not; where a folder records no limit, a flight that ran to a shorter one or ended early without losing the car is not C2; and a shakedown is not C2 (the operator marks it '_shake' in its tag). 3 flights per backend; every flight on its own line and the range over the backend's C2 flights; a flight that lost the car is a C2 flight, not a discard; a value a run did not record is shown as '-', never as 0. The detector supplies the hint, and while it has no box the model is not asked: a C2 score is the model's and the detector's together.

Fastest overall: bf16/sdpa/merged. Rule: low-VRAM option = lowest p50 among configurations with p50 <= gate, no timed parse failure and process VRAM (vram_process_gb) <= vram_budget_gb; fidelity option = highest exact agreement with the reference among the other configurations with p50 <= gate and no timed parse failure; neither is a default (budget 10 GB, an assumption until CityLife's VRAM is measured).

Why there is no default, and what each option costs (decided 2026-10-22, review of V1a). The precision changes what the model says: bf16 reproduces the upstream-precision reference - bf16/sdpa/unmerged gives the reference's output on 96 % of the 48 probe inputs, the merged bf16 rows on 88-90 % (greedy near-ties) - while every nf4 row agrees on only 25-33 % exactly and 48-50 % within 3 bins; 4-bit quantisation, not the attention kernel or the merge, is what moves the outputs. On the no-hint phrase every bf16 row and both nf4 merged rows answer '00 49 49 LAND' on 5 of 6 frames, while the nf4 unmerged loader (the one every earlier AerialVLA script here used) reproduces two of the 4 Aug malformed landing outputs ('00 49 4949', '00 49 494 49'); the strict reader turns those into holds. nf4/sdpa/merged is kept as the measured low-VRAM option (p50 517 ms; about 7.9 GB held by the process, 5.4 GB of it allocated by torch), not as a default that silently changes behaviour. A bf16 process holds about 16.8-16.9 GB: bf16/sdpa/unmerged (p50 486 ms) is the closest to the reference, bf16/sdpa/merged the fastest (p50 259 ms, below the paper's 0.35 s; its nvidia-smi reading failed, so its process VRAM is not recorded). The 10 GB budget behind the low-VRAM rule is an assumption, not a measurement: the card has 24 GB, desktop applications held about 3.4 GB before the runs (one reading, subtracted from every row's nvidia-smi figure), OWL-ViT takes 0.61 GB, and CityLife's own VRAM has never been logged. Measuring CityLife's VRAM beside the model is what decides which configuration flies (next wave); until then the server loads none by default.

## What R1, C1 and C2 are

- **R1 (rate, open loop):** deploy/evidence/vla_protocol_frames/*.png (224x448 front-over-down mosaics, Project AirSim, poses in frames.json); OpenVLA-7B is given the front (upper) 224x224 half; 5 warm-up, 50 timed inferences, the model's process alone on the GPU: no simulator, no second model. Reference: the grant's 10 Hz VLA -> Shield rate (100 ms). Floor: the stub on the same host.
- **C1 (closed loop):** policies/urban_demo_policy.yaml; instruction 'fly to (40, 40) at 6 m/s altitude 20'; spawn (0, 0); the policy's no-fly zone lies on the straight line; 5 seeds, 120 s limit, reached within 3.0 m. none: goal blend 0, no planner, no path follower. Assisted runs are recorded as assisted and are not C1. Nulls: hover (zero action): reached 0/N by construction; stub P-controller, given the target coordinates: the no-learning reference a learned backend has to beat.
- **C2 (closed-loop follow, added 2026-11-16; amended 2026-11-17, after the C2 flights of 16 November: a second null, the hand-written estimator follow (FAMILY=estimator), and its words (estimator_null_as_flown); nothing else in C2 changed, and the protocol id vla-backend-protocol/2 is unchanged, so a table built before this date carries C2 without it):** the Project AirSim follow: 'follow a red car' in CityLife under the perception node's default signed bundle (follow_car_citylife), cruise altitude 8 m; the mission clock starts when the start gate passes. With --identity, as C2 flies, the gate is follow_vlm.Reacquirer with no anchor: 10 identity-ok boxes in a row within 30 m (twice the node's --start-when-seen 5), of one object (each within 3 m + 3 m/s x the time since the last pick) and seen moving, an inference with no acceptable box or a box of another place restarting the streak, a place seen not to move refused; its streak and refusals are metrics.json start_gate.identity_gate. A run flown with --identity whose metrics.json start_gate records no `rule` was flown before the node stated this rule: its [start] line printed the pixel gate's streak and a timeout's end_reason reads '5 inferences in a row', though the rule above is the one that decided; hil: Project AirSim and ArduPilot SITL on the desktop; MAVROS 2, the perception node (demo/pas_ardupilot/perception_node.py --pilot vla --mission follow), the Shield and the VLA server on the Jetson Orin. Reported: frac_within_30m: share of the mission's ticks with the aircraft within 30 m of the car's true position (metrics.json; the simulator's pose, evaluation only); decision_hz_mission: answers the model parsed per second over the mission; decision_hz_asked: the same while it was being asked (not held by the rail, not withheld because the detector had no box); interventions_per_100_ticks; p0_escapes (must be 0); ticks, end_reason; the flight's own KPI verdict, from its kpi.json: outcome (success or fail) with mission_fail_reasons; unsafe_p0_ticks, the ticks the aircraft was in a position a P0 rule calls unsafe (position-based: 0 P0 escapes does not imply 0 of these); p0_ticks_not_measurable and p0_ticks_blind, the ticks on which the escape count could not be measured. Nulls: the hand-written follow on the same rail (--pilot servo, FAMILY=servo): the no-learning reference as flown - the perception node's FollowPilot, a box-width P-servo with no target estimator, no target lock and no lost end; the hand-written estimator follow on the same rail (--pilot servo --target-estimator, FAMILY=estimator; added 2026-11-17, after the C2 flights of 16 November): the lab's own no-learning follow - the perception node's EstimatorFollowPilot, follow_vlm.fly()'s servo on the target estimate with its strict lock and re-acquisition and its trail-following forward direction - kept apart from the box-servo null, with no lost end; not all of fly(), so not equal to the lab's follow. C2 was added on 16 November 2026, after the lab named AIoT-VLA v10b for the follow task on 15 November 2026. It was added because that choice rests on a follow, and C1 is a GOTO to a coordinate that a follow-only model cannot fly. A test written after the choice it bears on is not a prediction: C2 describes the chosen backend, and compares it only with backends that flew the same test.
- **Changes from vla-backend-protocol/1:** R1 and C1 are unchanged. C2 is new. The table's Orin rate column also takes benches that are not R1, each labelled with what makes it so. R1 is stated not to cover a backend served over protocol v2: its Orin timing is its server's bench.
