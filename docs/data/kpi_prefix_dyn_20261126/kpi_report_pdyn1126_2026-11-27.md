## Definitions, denominators and nulls

- **Family** - one row per (scenario template, parameter cell) and Shield arm (Stress Testing p6). Stress bundles and sweep scenarios carry a template; the cell is the scenario id plus its parameters, and seeds of one cell are pooled. Flights carry no template: their family is rail / policy / mission, and the cell is whether the policy was hot-applied in flight and where a declared subject stood. AirSim follow flights of one policy share a row: they declare no cell. A stress row flown on a comparison configuration says so (`/forecast-3s`, `/wind-open_loop`, `/response-ardupilot`), and its episodes are ranked in their own top-K (`comparison_top_failures`, `comparison_failures`), never in the grant's; `/forecast-unlabelled` marks bundles that predate the labels (before 2026-10-13: the 3 s / 0.5 s forecast and open-loop wind), not the grant's configuration. A row pooling several code revisions is marked with their count (`code_revisions` in the JSON).
- **P0 escape rate** - P0 escapes / ticks, pooled over the family's ticks. An escape is a P0 violation still present in the action FLOWN (`emitted_violations`). Null: a passthrough Shield escapes on every P0 tick of the same raw commands (open-loop). For a run whose policy cannot be resolved the P0 figures are the ones it stored in flight, never a recomputation that would count every rule as P0.
- **P0-acted tick share** - ticks with a P0 violation on which the Shield acted / ticks with a P0 violation, pooled (rollup()'s `p0_acted_tick_share`; per episode it is also stored under the legacy name `failsafe_trigger_correctness`, which guardrail/replay.py verifies). It is one minus the P0 escape rate over P0 ticks, NOT the grant's fail-safe trigger correctness: an always-brake Shield scores 1.0 here. The grant's KPI is the next column.
- **Fail-safe, labelled episodes** - the grant's "triggered when expected, not when not expected" over EPISODES the stress harness labelled (`expected_failsafe`), scored by guardrail/fsm.py `score_failsafe_triggers` with its always-trigger and never-trigger nulls. Where both this and the tick figure exist, this is the one that matches the grant's wording. In this report the labels are the ADOPTED reading (r2) of docs/data/kpi_prefix_dyn_20261126/rescore_r2_pdyn1126.json (--rescore-rail): a run that reading leaves out, or a Shield-off control, has no label here; every reading is in "Fail-safe trigger correctness by label reading". Since 2026-11-15 "triggered" is the FSM entering RTL or Land, or the autopilot's GeoFence taking the aircraft to RTL / LAND on a fence breach while the FSM held in Brake or Loiter (`failsafe_by` "autopilot_geofence"): the backstop's triggers are counted beside the figure, and the figure without them is printed beside it.
- **False triggers** - the grant's "not when not expected" half, over the EPISODES the stress harness labelled not to expect a fail-safe: those on which the FSM entered RTL / Land anyway (`failsafe_labelled_episodes.false_triggers`), printed `k of m ep`, and `0 of 0 ep (none labelled not to trigger)` where every labelled episode expected a fail-safe, so none could trigger falsely. Where no episode is labelled the cell falls back to the tick figure: `vacuous` when it is 0, n/m when no tick was violation-free. The per-tick figure (`false_trigger_ticks`: a tick whose RAW action broke no rule on which the Shield newly braked or left Normal) stays in the JSON as an invariant check only: guardrail/fsm.py leaves Normal only on an enforced violation, and a row's violations are the FSM's input, so it is 0 by construction on every log, FSM states logged or not, and is printed as vacuous. A non-zero would be a logging fault, not a false trigger.
- **Repair success** - (converged + converged via ClearanceEscape) / (ticks with a repair attempt - ticks whose log cannot say). Failures: fell through to Brake / Loiter, escalated to RTL / Land (`fsm_state_after`), flew a still-violating action, or converged with a repair larger than theta (2.0 m lateral / 0.5 m vertical, Safety Shield p4). Theta is applied only where the row gives a size in metres: the FSM's `fsm.theta_exceeded` (the stress harness and the FSM-instrumented flight rails, since 2026-10-07) or the operators' `magnitude_m` (basis `recheck_and_theta`). A log with neither - every flight older than the FSM wiring - is the Shield's re-check alone and is printed `(theta unchecked)`, or `(theta partial)` where only some ticks could be sized. `none attempted` = zero attempts; `shield off` = repairs logged on a control arm and never flown. Null: an always-brake Shield scores 0 %. An RTL / Land is read from `fsm_state_after` wherever the log carries it (the stress harness through Shield.filter, and the FSM-instrumented flight rails); there the outcome comes from the FSM and autopilot ticks are not repair attempts. `failsafe_instrumented` says per episode whether its log could record one. Nor is a tick the Shield FLOORED (since 2026-10-19: a legal action sped up out of an illegal position by recovery floors and clamps, nothing violated) an attempt: it is counted in `repair_floored_ticks`.
- **Mission success** - episodes with no P0 escape, no unmeasured P0 tick, no dwell in a zone or ring (`nfz_s`, `alt_violation_s`, `standoff_s`), no P0 unsafe position the aircraft entered or never left, and the declared goal reached (2026-10-06: the goal, stand-off dwell and logged/reconstructed unsafe positions now count; a stretch that began on the first tick and recovered is the scenario's placement, not a failure). Stored tables keep their own verdict with the goal re-applied. Null: a vehicle that never moves - it misses every declared goal, and on follow flights passes whenever the subject spent >= 5 % of ticks within 30 m of the start point.
- **Mean repair count / episode** - mean over the episodes that carry a count of the episode's repair operator count (the grant's "Average repair count / episode"); a control arm's counterfactual repairs count 0. A passthrough Shield scores 0 - and escapes.
- **Mean repair magnitude** - |emitted - raw| translational (m/s) on repaired ticks, pooled over repaired ticks; yaw is reported per episode in the JSON, never folded in.
- **Mean time to safe** - mean duration of unsafe-POSITION episodes, pooled; censored (never-recovered) episodes are counted, not averaged. Logs without `unsafe` are reconstructed from the pose where the policy resolves; a dynamic (hot-applied) run cannot be and stays n/m.
- **KPI-grade** - `guardrail.manifest.is_kpi_grade` on the manifest (hil topology with its evidence, sim_speedup 1.0, a clean revision), AND the replay bundle beside the run verified with a trusted signature, its KPIs re-derived from its own log (`guardrail.replay.kpi_evidence_grade`), and holding the run folder's own flight_log.jsonl and manifest.json (`bundle_binding`). A run whose manifest passes but whose bundle is missing, unsigned, untrusted or another flight's is not KPI-grade (`kpi_grade_manifest` keeps the manifest's verdict).
- **Vacuous** - a 0 that counts an event which could not happen: an escape rate where no raw command violated a P0 rule, the per-tick false-trigger count (0 by construction on every log; rollup()'s `vacuous_zeros` says whether the logs were stateless or carried the FSM's states). Printed as vacuous, never as a measured 0.
- **Repair magnitude by priority / class** - the mean repair magnitude split by the highest priority a repaired tick violated (P0 / P1 / P2) and by the operator classes that acted on it (the grant's AltitudeClamp, LateralProjection, PathRepair, and this Shield's extensions); `never` where a priority or class had no repaired tick. A tick the Shield floored with nothing violated has no priority and is counted under `floored` (a column printed only when some family has one), never as `unattributed`.
