## Definitions, denominators and nulls

- **Family** - one row per (scenario template, parameter cell) and Shield arm (Stress Testing p6). Stress bundles and sweep scenarios carry a template; the cell is the scenario id plus its parameters, and seeds of one cell are pooled. Flights carry no template: their family is rail / policy / mission, and the cell is whether the policy was hot-applied in flight and where a declared subject stood. AirSim follow flights of one policy share a row: they declare no cell. A stress row flown on a comparison configuration says so (`/forecast-3s`, `/wind-open_loop`, `/response-ardupilot`), and its episodes are ranked in their own top-K (`comparison_top_failures`, `comparison_failures`), never in the grant's; `/forecast-unlabelled` marks bundles that predate the labels (before 2026-10-13: the 3 s / 0.5 s forecast and open-loop wind), not the grant's configuration. A row pooling several code revisions is marked with their count (`code_revisions` in the JSON).
- **P0 escape rate** - P0 escapes / ticks, pooled over the family's ticks. An escape is a P0 violation still present in the action FLOWN (`emitted_violations`). Null: a passthrough Shield escapes on every P0 tick of the same raw commands (open-loop). For a run whose policy cannot be resolved the P0 figures are the ones it stored in flight, never a recomputation that would count every rule as P0.
- **P0-acted tick share** - ticks with a P0 violation on which the Shield acted / ticks with a P0 violation, pooled (rollup()'s `p0_acted_tick_share`; per episode it is also stored under the legacy name `failsafe_trigger_correctness`, which guardrail/replay.py verifies). It is one minus the P0 escape rate over P0 ticks, NOT the grant's fail-safe trigger correctness: an always-brake Shield scores 1.0 here. The grant's KPI is the next column.
- **Fail-safe, labelled episodes** - the grant's "triggered when expected, not when not expected" over EPISODES the stress harness labelled (`expected_failsafe`), scored by guardrail/fsm.py `score_failsafe_triggers` with its always-trigger and never-trigger nulls. Where both this and the tick figure exist, this is the one that matches the grant's wording. In this report the labels are the ADOPTED reading (r2) of docs/data/gazebo_sweep_20261124/rescore_rail_gz.json (--rescore-rail): a run that reading leaves out, or a Shield-off control, has no label here; every reading is in "Fail-safe trigger correctness by label reading". Since 2026-11-15 "triggered" is the FSM entering RTL or Land, or the autopilot's GeoFence taking the aircraft to RTL / LAND on a fence breach while the FSM held in Brake or Loiter (`failsafe_by` "autopilot_geofence"): the backstop's triggers are counted beside the figure, and the figure without them is printed beside it.
- **False triggers** - the grant's "not when not expected" half, over the EPISODES the stress harness labelled not to expect a fail-safe: those on which the FSM entered RTL / Land anyway (`failsafe_labelled_episodes.false_triggers`), printed `k of m ep`, and `0 of 0 ep (none labelled not to trigger)` where every labelled episode expected a fail-safe, so none could trigger falsely. Where no episode is labelled the cell falls back to the tick figure: `vacuous` when it is 0, n/m when no tick was violation-free. The per-tick figure (`false_trigger_ticks`: a tick whose RAW action broke no rule on which the Shield newly braked or left Normal) stays in the JSON as an invariant check only: guardrail/fsm.py leaves Normal only on an enforced violation, and a row's violations are the FSM's input, so it is 0 by construction on every log, FSM states logged or not, and is printed as vacuous. A non-zero would be a logging fault, not a false trigger.
- **Repair success** - (converged + converged via ClearanceEscape) / (ticks with a repair attempt - ticks whose log cannot say). Failures: fell through to Brake / Loiter, escalated to RTL / Land (`fsm_state_after`), flew a still-violating action, or converged with a repair larger than theta (2.0 m lateral / 0.5 m vertical, Safety Shield p4). ## End-to-end tick budget, per tick

Safety Shield p6: "Monitor tick budget <= 100 ms (10 Hz) end-to-end". Every flown row's own timing over the whole flight, pooled per rail (nearest rank), KPI-grade episodes apart from the rest. A measure no row and no metrics.json carries is "not measured", never 0.

- `tick_ms`: tick start to the setpoint's publication: the node's own work per tick.
- `e2e_ms`: an action's receipt to the first setpoint flown from it (hil Shield node; since 2026-11-11 the tick runs on the action's arrival: a deferred or timer tick adds the wait for its poll).
- `e2e_with_wait_ms`: e2e_ms plus the wait the action may have had in the DDS queue behind the previous tick's hot-apply bookkeeping (hil Shield node since 2026-11-23, blocked_by_deferred_ms, an upper bound): the budget's end-to-end time, so work moved out of the tick still counts.
- `cam_age_ms`: age of the camera frame behind the flown action at its publication (perception rail, VLA pilot), every tick a VLA decision's action flew.
- `cam_to_setpoint_ms`: camera frame to the FIRST setpoint flown from its decision (perception rail, VLA pilot): camera input to action output.

Clocks: each measure is the difference of two readings of ONE clock - the hil Shield node's `time.monotonic_ns()`, the perception node's loop clock (the frame's arrival stamp, the tick and the publication all read it). On the perception rail the camera frame's time is its ARRIVAL at the node (the pose ring there carries no simulator stamps), so render and transport before it are not in `cam_*`: a lower bound of camera to action.
